# Surface brief: ISP marketing landing page

Mode: Persuade. Audience: H&S managers and ops leaders at high-hazard operations comparing QHSE vendors. Action: book a free gap assessment (primary) or a demo. Proof available: product UI rendered from the shadcn kit with synthetic, labelled data; no customer proof yet. Constraints: brief-pinned palette, light-first with navy sections, no invented claims, WCAG 2.2 AA, Lighthouse 95+.

Direction source: brief-pinned (owner-approved brief + research.md). No concept roll was run because the brief fixes the world; build path is code-led (no image generation available).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page proves the closed loop instead of claiming it. The visitor watches a finding become a CAPA that only turns "Verified closed" once someone verifies it, then sees the tangle of tools resolve into one chain. It refuses the category default: centred stock-worker photo over a grid of module icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Paper and white grounds with full-width Navy fields at the problem, maturity and final-CTA beats. Blue is the only action colour; orange/green/red appear only as status. Geist for everything, Geist Mono only for record IDs, timestamps and figures. Hairline Line borders, 12px panels, 8px controls, pills only for status badges. Product surfaces are real kit components in light window frames with soft navy-tinted shadows.

STORY: Understand: ISP keeps one linked record from hazard to audit. Believe: CAPAs can't close unverified, crews can use it, it maps to the standards they're audited on, and nothing on the page is faked. Do: get the gap assessment.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 12-col grid, 1200px max. Left 5 cols: eyebrow, two-line H1 (~56px), 25-word sub, primary "Get a free gap assessment" + secondary "Watch 2-min demo", one trust line. Right 7 cols: layered composition, CAPA-142 detail panel in front, HIRAC 5×5 matrix offset top-right behind it, Pump-04 inspection finding card lower-left. Primary action visible above the fold at 1280×720.

FORM: Product-as-hero enterprise landing (Linear/Vanta grammar) with a scroll-driven record chain as the signature interaction. Seed: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
